Advisors named on a Barron's or Forbes ranking — about 9,000 people, or 2.3% of the universe. Both sources are pooled because what matters for prospecting is that an advisor is sizable and recognised, not who ranked them. Ranks are only comparable within the same list, and each badge names its own. Barron's is joined on CRD via BrokerCheck; Forbes publishes no CRD, so most of those are matched on name, firm and location and are marked ≈ on the map.
Advisors with an email address or a phone number on file — from the CRM export and 26 scraped firm rosters, matched to SEC CRDs by name within the firm. A record carrying neither is only a name and does not pass. About a third of matches are unconfirmed and marked ⚠ on the card; they still count as reachable, because the contact detail is real even where the identity is inferred.
Advisors carrying an asset figure from the CRM — either a team total or their own book. Coverage is thin and uneven: the CRM populates this for under 8% of contacts, so switching it on shows where EIC has recorded a number, NOT where the money is. Team amounts are stored once on the team and shown as the whole team's book, never as one person's.
From Form ADV Schedule A, collected for the full firm roster, so this covers every mapped firm rather than only those reporting Item 5.G(7). A holding below 5% is an officer rather than an owner and is tagged as such.
